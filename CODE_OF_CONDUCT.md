# Code of conduct

StagePane contributors are expected to make participation respectful, safe,
welcoming, and professionally useful.

We welcome people regardless of experience, identity, background, disability,
language, location, or employment. Be precise without being demeaning. Critique
ideas and code, not people. Respect consent and privacy. Do not publish private
communications, screen content, credentials, or identifying information.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, sustained
disruption, impersonation, doxxing, and retaliation are not accepted in project
spaces or when representing the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, temporarily limit
participation, or permanently ban a participant when proportionate to behavior
and community safety. Conflicts involving a maintainer must be escalated to an
independent reviewer when one is available.

Send conduct reports privately to
[support@hinoshiba.com](mailto:support@hinoshiba.com). Include only the context
needed to review the report, and do not copy private communications or personal
data that are unrelated to it. The project will publish a fuller enforcement
and appeal process as its governance matures.
